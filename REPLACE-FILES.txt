INSTALLATION
1. Back up your existing portfolio folder.
2. Copy these seven files into that folder, replacing the six matching files:
   index.html
   styles.css
   hero-3d.css
   nav-glass.css
   character-loop.js
   icon-orbit.js
   favicon.svg (new)
3. Keep your assets folder, script.js, nav-scroll.js and manifest.json.
4. Reload the page with a hard refresh. Use a local web server or your normal hosting to test SVG textures and manifest loading.
